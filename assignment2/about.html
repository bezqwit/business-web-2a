About Bez's Bakery

Our Story

Bez’s Bakery began in 2022 as a small family kitchen with one oven, a handful of handwritten recipes, and a dream to share something special with our community. What started as early mornings baking bread for neighbors quickly turned into long lines out the door and a growing family of loyal customers. Today, we’re proud to have expanded to multiple locations, but our heart remains the same. Every loaf, pastry, and cake is still made with the same care, tradition, and love that started it all. We believe great baking brings people together — whether it’s over morning coffee, birthday celebrations, or simply the comfort of something warm and fresh.

Our Mission

At Bez's Bakery, our mission is to craft pastries and baked goods using the highest quality ingredients, while supporting our community and creating memorable experiences for every customer.
